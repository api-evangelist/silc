MONROVIA, Calif.–(BUSINESS WIRE)–SiLC Technologies, Inc. (SiLC) today announced Eyeonic® Vista (Vista), an ultra-long-range, high-resolution 4D vision system. Vista introduces a new benchmark in long-range sensing, combining precision, robustness, and intelligent target analysis in a compact, ruggedized platform.
